Costs & prices

Recipe prices and estimated ingredient costs. Check your recipe records before relying on these figures.

Shopping list

A starting list for one batch of each matched menu dish. Check quantities and units before shopping.

How this list works

Recipes are matched to menu dishes by name. Dishes without a matching recipe are omitted. Ingredients with the same name may be combined even if their units differ. Sales and stock do not change this list.

Money

Estimates for all recorded sales. Check missing costs before relying on totals.

How these estimates work

Totals use current recipe prices and ingredient costs where a recipe matches. Sales without matching recipes have no ingredient cost included. Other expenses are not included, so figures may be incomplete and are not net profit.
